Letter From The Club President

Dear Delegates,

It is my greatest honor to welcome you all to the first-ever edition of the Şehit Erol Olçok Anatolian High School Model United Nations Conference — ŞEOMUN’26, taking place from January 22nd to 25th. What started as a small vision among a few passionate students has now turned into a remarkable project—one that reflects dedication, belief, and the shared effort of an entire community. As delegates, you will have the chance to engage with issues that stretch across different times and disciplines, discovering in the process not only the complexities of global affairs but also the potential within yourselves.
To our Secretariat, Academic, and Organizational Teams—your perseverance and commitment have been nothing short of inspiring. You have turned an idea into a living reality, and for that, you deserve every bit of recognition and gratitude. Dear delegates, as you step into this experience, I invite you to approach every session with an open mind and a strong spirit. Let these four days be a journey where discussions turn into friendships, and where inspiration meets ambition.
Welcome to ŞEOMUN’26. May this conference become a memory that continues to motivate you long after it ends. Warm regards,
Yusuf İslam İlkis

MUN Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about participating in Model United Nations

What is a Model United Nations (MUN)?

MUN is an educational simulation where students role-play as delegates of the United Nations. Participants debate global issues, draft resolutions, and improve skills in diplomacy, negotiation, and public speaking.

Who can participate in a MUN conference?

Any high school student interested in international relations, public speaking, or debate can join. Delegates are usually grouped by high school and assigned a country or committee to represent.

Do I need prior experience to join MUN?

No prior experience is necessary. Beginners can learn the basics of MUN through workshops and guides provided by the conference organizers.

Is there free accommadition?

No prior experience is necessary. Beginners can learn the basics of MUN through workshops and guides provided by the conference organizers.

What is expected from delegates during sessions?

Delegates are expected to research their assigned country, actively participate in debates, propose solutions, negotiate with other delegates, and contribute to drafting resolutions.

Where can I find resources to prepare for MUN?

Many conferences provide delegate handbooks and research guides. Online resources, MUN blogs, and YouTube channels are also useful for learning parliamentary procedure, debate skills, and country positions.
